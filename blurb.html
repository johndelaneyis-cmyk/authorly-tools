On the morning her sister's body washes ashore, Marina realizes she should never have come back to the island.

Setup

Fifteen years after fleeing her childhood island, marine biologist Marina Whelan returns home for a funeral she suspects shouldn't be one. The locals are saying nothing. The same storm that took her sister has begun pulling other things to the surface — including a body she last saw alive in 2008.

Stakes

The longer Marina stays, the deeper she sinks into a current of complicity that runs through every house in the harbor. To get the truth, she'll have to risk what little family she has left.

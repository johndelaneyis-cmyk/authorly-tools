Three categories to request

1. Cozy Bookshop Mystery

  • Path: Kindle Store › Kindle eBooks › Mystery, Thriller & Suspense › Mystery › Cozy › Cats & Dogs
  • Why it fits: Bookshop cozies, female amateur sleuth, small-town setting, cold-case angle.
  • Competition: Light — top 100 typically has 200–2,000 reviews.
  • Top-100 likelihood: Easy

2. Women Sleuths

  • Path: Kindle Store › Kindle eBooks › Mystery, Thriller & Suspense › Mystery › Women Sleuths
  • Why it fits: Forty-something widow protagonist, character-driven mystery, atmospheric.
  • Competition: Moderate — 500–5,000 reviews at #100.
  • Top-100 likelihood: Moderate

3. Small Town & Rural Fiction

  • Path: Kindle Store › Kindle eBooks › Literature & Fiction › Genre Fiction › Small Town & Rural
  • Why it fits: Maine setting, community focus, light romance subplot.
  • Competition: Heavy — top 100 has trad-pub names with 50K+ reviews.
  • Top-100 likelihood: Stretch

How to add these

  • Email kdp-support with subject "Add categories — ASIN [your ASIN]" and paste the paths verbatim. Amazon assigns 2 by default; the other 8 slots come from this email.

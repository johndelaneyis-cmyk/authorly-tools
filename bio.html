Long bio (130–170 words)

Marina Whelan writes literary mysteries rooted in the coastal communities of Atlantic Canada. After a decade studying ocean ecosystems at the Bedford Institute of Oceanography, she traded the deck of a research vessel for the discipline of a writing desk — though the salt and weather of her former life shape every page she writes.

Her debut, The Salt Tide, follows a marine biologist who returns to her childhood island to investigate her sister's mysterious death. It comes out in spring 2026 with Random House Canada.

Marina lives in Halifax, Nova Scotia, with her husband, their two daughters, and a kayak named Persephone. She is happiest at low tide.

Title + description in. Six tested-shape ad headlines out — three at the standard 100–150 character size, three short ones at 60–80 characters for tighter placements. Each variant tries a different angle (trope, comp, emotional, audience).

Standard headlines (100–150 chars)

  1. "On the morning her sister's body washes ashore, marine biologist Marina returns to the island that destroyed her family." (128/150)
  2. "An atmospheric coastal mystery for fans of Tana French and Lisa Jewell. Slow-burn dread, dual timelines, one small island." (124/150)
  3. "Fifteen years after she fled, the truth her sister hid is about to surface. A cold-case mystery wrapped in salt water." (118/150)

Short headlines (60–80 chars)

  1. "An atmospheric coastal mystery for fans of Tana French." (56/80)
  2. "Some islands keep their dead. Some keep their secrets." (54/80)
  3. "Marina came home for a funeral. She found a murder." (52/80)

Why these work

Variant 1 (standard) leads with the hook line. Variant 2 names comps for fan-targeting ads. Variant 3 frames the central mystery hook. Each short variant trims a different angle so you can A/B by audience instead of just length.

# Cold-email templates — paste-ready

Three audiences. Replace `[NAME]`, `[NEWSLETTER/CHANNEL/HANDLE]`, and `[ONE-SENTENCE-PERSONALIZATION]` per recipient.

Personalization tip: read 1 recent piece they published, mention it in one sentence at the open. Generic templates get spam-filtered. Specific ones get opened.

I'm an indie author. I built a free toolkit at **authorly.tools** — six tools for the actually-painful parts of self-publishing: comp title finder, Amazon blurb writer, KDP keyword expander, category recommender, trope finder, Amazon Ads headlines, author bio generator. No signup. No manuscript upload. 5 free runs per tool per day per visitor (covers most authors' launch needs).

Why I'm emailing: would your readers want to know about it? It's free, no affiliate strings, no email harvesting — just a useful free thing. If you'd like a no-strings link drop in [NEWSLETTER], I'd be grateful. If sponsored placement makes more sense for your business model, I can budget for that too — happy to discuss what that looks like.

Either way: would you tell me which tools you'd actually use? Output quality is my main concern and feedback from someone who covers KDP at your depth would be gold.

Darren

**Audience:** BookTok creators with indie-author audiences. Find them by searching `#indieauthor`, `#kdp`, `#selfpublishing` on TikTok.

Subject lines don't really exist for TikTok DMs. Just open with the message.

**Message (TikTok DM or Instagram DM, max ~500 chars):**

Hi [NAME] — I'm an indie author and I built a free toolkit for KDP launches at authorly.tools. Six tools: comp finder, blurb writer, KDP keywords, etc. Free, no signup, paid for the AI bill myself.

Do you ever review/demo author tools? If you'd want to test it on a real launch and post a "is this any good" video, I'd love to see what you find. No agreement implied — just genuinely want feedback from someone whose audience is indie authors.

## How to find email addresses

- **Newsletter editors:** their newsletter usually has a "contact us" or the editor's email in the footer. If not, try `editor@[domain]` or `hi@[domain]`.
- **YouTubers:** "About" tab → "Email for business inquiries" — many list one. If not, their newsletter signup often has the same email.
- **BookTok creators:** TikTok DMs work. Instagram DMs work. Email rarely listed.

Don't buy lists. Don't blast 100 emails. **Send 5-10 personalized cold emails on launch day** and reply to whatever bounces back. That'll outperform 200 generic ones every time.
